## UI conventions

- The habit tracker (`app/(authed)/habit/`) and nav rail (`components/IconRail.tsx`) reflect the June 2026 UX refresh and supersede the original wireframes — do not revert them toward the old prototypes.
- Icons come from `lucide-react` — do not reintroduce hand-rolled inline SVGs.
- Baloo 2 (headings/numbers) has no Thai glyphs; Thai text must stay on Noto Sans Thai.
